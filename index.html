Customer Intelligence Platform

Built an end-to-end churn analytics platform for 7,043 telecom customers. Combined exploratory analysis, statistical testing, predictive modeling, customer risk segmentation, and retention-focused decision support.

Compared Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forest, and an ANN rather than assuming the most complex model would perform best. Tuned Random Forest achieved the highest test ROC-AUC at 0.8441 and was retained as the final model.

Intelligent Business Forecasting

Built a sales forecasting platform using historical retail data, chronological model validation, feature engineering, future forecasting, and business-facing visualization.

Compared Random Forest with a 30-day-sequence LSTM on the same 76-day held-out test period. Random Forest achieved 6.03% MAPE versus 14.73% for the LSTM and was retained as the final model.
